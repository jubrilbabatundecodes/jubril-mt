### Instruction
- Create 10 questions asking for every phrase that matches a specified part of speech (such as nouns or verbs) or named-entity type (such as people or dates) in a particular paragraph.
- Identify each paragraph by its order ("first," "second," and so on) and a short description of its subject.
- Make each question clear enough for an elementary-school student to locate the paragraph.
- You may ask more than one question about a paragraph.
- Tell the reader to list answers separated by commas, then provide the correct answer after each question.

### Format
Use this structure for each item:
Type: NER/POS
Question: ...
Answer: ...

### Example
Type: POS
Question: List all the nouns in the first paragraph. Your answers should be separated by a comma.
Answer: Pick, run, go, sell, buy, love.

Type: NER
Question: List all the organizations in the third paragraph that mention Susan. Your answers should be separated by a comma.
Answer: World Health Organization, Harvard University.
