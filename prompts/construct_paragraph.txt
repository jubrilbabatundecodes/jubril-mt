### Instruction
Write a fictional {media} about {topic} in approximately 250-300 words. Invent the people, places, and objects; do not present them as real historical entities.

Use this process:
1. **Invent**: Create original characters, settings, and objects that have no basis in real historical entities.

2. **Plan**: Briefly organize the main ideas and events.

3. **Draft**: Write the piece using your plan and stay within the requested length.

4. **Revise**: Improve clarity, originality, and flow, and check grammar and spelling.

5. **Finalize**: Return a polished, engaging version.

Return only the finished piece.

### Format
Follow this format:
Topic: ...
{content}
